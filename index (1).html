Our Story

More than a haircut

Sharp Cuts was born from a simple belief — that every man deserves a barbershop where craft, respect, and community come first. We're not a chain, not a franchise. We're your neighborhood shop in Raynham, and we take that seriously.

From classic fades to modern textured cuts, our barbers bring precision to every chair. Walk in a regular, leave looking exceptional.
